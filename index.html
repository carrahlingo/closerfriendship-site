Quick capture

Capture it in seconds.

Tap +, type or talk, and pick their name. No hunting for the right screen, so the detail gets saved before you forget it.

Nudges

Check in on your terms.

Weekly for your best friend, every few months for your college crew. Set a rhythm for each person, and Closer nudges you when it's time. Every Sunday, you'll get a preview of the week's nudges and occasions.

Profiles

Everything about them, in one place.

Life updates, family, where they live, and when you last caught up, all on one page instead of scattered across your apps.

Tags & occasions

Remember what they love.

Tag hobbies and friend groups so you can find everyone in your book club or who loves to hike. And save the birthdays and anniversaries that matter to them.
